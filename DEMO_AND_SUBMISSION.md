# 2-minute demo script
1. (15s) Problem: reps waste time on the wrong leads; most CRMs give a score with no reason.
2. (30s) Load sample CRM. Show Ranked tab: score + the exact fields and points behind it.
3. (30s) Click "Re-rank with AI": the LLM reads the free-text notes (e.g. "CFO asked for contract terms") that keyword rules miss; show the AI reason on a lead.
4. (20s) Approvals: approve one action, show the drafted email. Nothing sends without approval.
5. (15s) Cleanup tab: duplicates and stale leads. Digest tab: today's top 5.
6. (10s) Evaluation tab: precision@10 versus simple baselines, with the honest note on synthetic labels.
